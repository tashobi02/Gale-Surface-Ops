How to read it:

- The generated block holds every DataSource record in `pipeline/sites/gale_boxwork/sources/` and every POI image. Credit, licence and URL are the record's own words; nothing in it is typed here. To change one, change the record and run `python3 scripts/build_credits.py`.
- Where a publisher's page states no licence, the record says so with the URL and the date checked, or names the publisher's general media policy (BD-13).
- The sections after the block cover what has no DataSource record: the derived layers, the storm-replay inputs that the build does not ship, and the partner-agency credits.

## Disagreements between 02 and the records

Neither side is changed here. They were reported to the story lead and the data lead; none blocks the credits.

1. **L1 title.** 02 L1: "USGS *Mars MSL Gale Merged Orthophoto Mosaic 25 cm* v3 (Calef & Parker 2016), grayscale 23 GB lossless GeoTIFF; colour HiRISE LRGB COG (~1 GB) also published". Record `usgs-gale-ortho-25cm-v3`: title "Mars MSL Gale Merged Orthophoto Mosaic 25cm, colour version HiRISE 78 quad color basemap", authors "Parker and Calef", and a `version` that says the file name carries no version number. The credit and use-constraint strings match 02.
2. **L2 credit.** 02 L2 has no attribution line. The record's credit is "Please credit authors"; [data-downloads.md](data-downloads.md#b1-terrain-and-imagery-tier-0-critical-path) gives a longer line for D1 to D3 that includes "Parker & Calef 2016". The UI shows the record's credit.
3. **L4 tile and unit.** 02 L4 gives the 30S120E pattern with the file name "to confirm"; the record and [coverage.md](feasibility/coverage.md#themis-quantitative-thermal-inertia-l4-d4) use the published `THEMIS_TI_Mosaic_Quant_30S120E_100mpp`. 02 L4 says the unit is tiu; the cube's label names none, so tiu is an assumption (BD-26, in the record).
4. **L6 traverse identifier.** 02 L6 names doi:10.17189/1520397, which belongs to the older PDS3 data set; the PDS4 bundle read here carries doi:10.17189/8nyv-8p60 (record `pds-msl-places-localizations`).
5. **L7 credit wording.** 02 L7 writes "Hassler et al. 2014" and "Simonsen et al. 2024" (no comma); the records write "Hassler et al., 2014" and "Simonsen et al., 2024". The generated block shows the records.
6. **`apollo17-planning` and `tobler-1993`.** The record `apollo17-planning` describes an Apollo 15 document although the source id and 02 name Apollo 17, and its URL answered HTTP 404 on 2026-11-12; `tobler-1993` describes the 2023 paper by Wood et al. at the 02 Sources URL, not Tobler's 1993 work. Each record's `accuracy_note` says so.
