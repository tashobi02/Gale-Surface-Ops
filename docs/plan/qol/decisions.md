| ID | Item | Decision | Rationale | Label |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| QD-001 | Q2: who may hold whom | `eva:EVn` may name only its own `crew_id` (a different one is `FORBIDDEN_ROLE`); `ops` may name either. A missing `crew_id` is `INVALID`, as for `SUMMON_VEHICLE` `crew` | Same "self" convention as SUMMON_VEHICLE and ACK_ALERT in `commands.ts` | decided by the orchestrator (Claude) under Sadman Shaharier Mahim's authority, 13 Nov 2026 |
| QD-002 | Q2: when HOLD is possible | Only for a crew member who is `WALKING` or `AT_STOP` and has left the hab. `IN_HAB`, `SHELTERED`, `TO_HAVEN` and a crew member still waiting out `start_offset_s` get `INVALID`; outside `RUNNING` and `PAUSED` it is `BUSY`. A repeated `HOLD`, or a `RESUME` when not `HOLDING`, is `INVALID` and logs nothing | A hold must never stop a crew member who is heading for shelter; the UI flips its label from the snapshot, so a refused repeat is harmless | decided by the orchestrator (Claude) under Sadman Shaharier Mahim's authority, 13 Nov 2026 |
| QD-003 | Q2: what keeps running while held | Position and walk progress (the engine `motion` record, including the stop dwell timer) freeze. O₂, dose and EVA time keep running, at the resting rate in the suit (the same mode as a held crew member at the end of a route) | A standing crew member rests; consumables and dose still run, as the brief requires | decided by the orchestrator (Claude) under Sadman Shaharier Mahim's authority, 13 Nov 2026 |
| QD-004 | Q2: what RESUME restores | `AT_STOP` when the hold interrupted a stop dwell (the motion phase is `dwell`), otherwise `WALKING`; the walk continues from the held point with no re-anchoring | Needs no extra stored field: the reducer reads the motion record | decided by the orchestrator (Claude) under Sadman Shaharier Mahim's authority, 13 Nov 2026 |
| QD-005 | Q2: a held crew member in other engine rules | Counts as out on the surface for the link graph, haven time, contingency options and `crew.next_stop_*` (bearing and distance stay; the ETA is `null` while held, as for a held crew member). It is left out of the crew a `JUMP_NEXT_STOP` waits for, because a held crew member never arrives | A hold must not hide a crew member from safety logic | decided by the orchestrator (Claude) under Sadman Shaharier Mahim's authority, 13 Nov 2026 |
| QD-006 | Q2: tablet layout | The top bar is a flex row: the alert slot takes the remaining width (`flex: 1 1 0`, its message truncates with an ellipsis and a `title`), the HOLD / RESUME button sits at the right with a "SIM" tag under it. Neutral chrome, label from `crew.activity` (`simulated`), disabled unless the crew member is walking, at a stop or holding. A refusal shows the Hab Core's message | Brief: shorten the alert bar from the right; colour, shape and word of the alert stay | decided by the orchestrator (Claude) under Sadman Shaharier Mahim's authority, 13 Nov 2026 |
| QD-007 | Q2: HUD | The HUD shows `HOLDING` in the bottom row only while the crew member is held, so the five-value rule of a normal HUD is unchanged | Brief: the HUD shows HOLDING as the activity | decided by the orchestrator (Claude) under Sadman Shaharier Mahim's authority, 13 Nov 2026 |
| QD-008 | Q3: boarding distance | **Boarding distance = the same routing-grid cell** as the vehicle. The ParamSets (`pr1-assumed`, `ur1-assumed`) hold no boarding distance, so none is invented; the README's own wording is "same cell". Without a terrain grid the command is `INVALID` (there is no vehicle route either). | No number without a source. `ingress_min` (5 min, 02 §D.8) is a time, not a distance, and is not applied here (as for a summon's sheltering, BD-58), so the ride time is exactly the drive time. | decided by the orchestrator (Claude) under Sadman Shaharier Mahim's authority, 13 Nov 2026 |
| QD-009 | Q3: a summon is not a ride start | **"Or the vehicle is summoned first" is not offered as a ride start (amended by QD-014: an arrived summon is).** A summon is the contingency flow: on arrival the crew member is sheltered in the vehicle (`CREW_SHELTERED`), which ends their walk. So the rider must be in the vehicle's cell (at the hab at the start, or holding beside it after a ride). The tablet button is enabled for an available rover and the Hab Core's refusal reason ("not at PR1") is shown. | Keeps the summon and the ride separate; a pick-up leg (vehicle drives to the crew member, then on) is a later extension, not in Q3. | decided by the orchestrator (Claude) under Sadman Shaharier Mahim's authority, 13 Nov 2026 |
| QD-010 | Q3: vehicle state and one ride at a time | The vehicle must be `PARKED` (else `BUSY`); a rider already aboard is `BUSY`. After `CREW_DISMOUNTED` the vehicle is `PARKED` at the stop, so it can be summoned or ridden again. | One ride at a time per vehicle; no new vehicle state. | decided by the orchestrator (Claude) under Sadman Shaharier Mahim's authority, 13 Nov 2026 |
| QD-011 | Q3: which stop | The stop may be any **later** stop of the plan in force (`stop_index` above the rider's last stop), with a leg that ends at it. Stops skipped on the way get no `ARRIVED_STOP`; their tasks stay open. | The command names `to_stop_id`; the tablet sends the next stop. | decided by the orchestrator (Claude) under Sadman Shaharier Mahim's authority, 13 Nov 2026 |
| QD-012 | Q3: arrival events | On arrival the rider gets `CREW_DISMOUNTED` **and** `ARRIVED_STOP` (system), so stop timing, tasks, the report and the event log see an ordinary arrival; the rider then dwells at the stop and walks on from the next leg. | `ARRIVED_STOP` is what the report and the ops log read. | decided by the orchestrator (Claude) under Sadman Shaharier Mahim's authority, 13 Nov 2026 |
| QD-013 | Q3: shielding and O2 while riding | While `RIDING` the shielding is the `pressurized_rover` factor and no suit O2 is used (the cabin is pressurized), as for a crew member sheltered in the vehicle. The rider is not a radio node and has no haven clock while aboard. A plan revision that sends the rider `TO_HAVEN` mid-ride is not specially handled: the vehicle finishes its drive and stays `ARRIVED`. | Reuses existing, sourced factors. Edge case noted; contingency while riding is out of scope for Q3. | decided by the orchestrator (Claude) under Sadman Shaharier Mahim's authority, 13 Nov 2026 |
| QD-014 | Q3: ride from an arrived summon (amends QD-009) | A crew member who is `SHELTERED` in the pressurized rover (its `SUMMON` task names them) or `HOLDING` beside it can `RIDE_VEHICLE` once the rover is `ARRIVED` at them after a `SUMMON_VEHICLE`: boarding counts as already aboard, the rover need not be `PARKED`, and the held crew member steps to the rendezvous point (no distance check; a rover without option has no crew named in its task, so a `HOLDING` crew member counts as the one it came for). Another named crew member still out keeps the rover `BUSY`. The flow hold, summon, go by rover works; everything else in QD-008 to QD-013 is unchanged. The SCR-12 button follows the same rule from the snapshot. The replay reference log hash is unchanged (no `HOLD` or `RIDE_VEHICLE` in it) | A held crew member cannot walk to the rendezvous, so requiring the same grid cell would strand them | decided by the orchestrator (Claude) under Sadman Shaharier Mahim's authority, 13 Nov 2026 |
| B04-01 | B-04: SCR-02 top bar over the drawer toggles on a 390 px phone | No layout change: the FD-425 fix (top bar wraps below 640 px; site name and data-version chip truncate) already clears both toggles. `release-matrix` gains a B-04 case for the "POI · Conditions" toggle and asserts that neither `.scr02-site-name` nor `.scr02-chip` overlaps either toggle's box | Re-run on `phone-portrait-390x844`: both toggles open their drawers, with no pointer interception, so a second fix would only add risk | decided by the orchestrator (Claude) under Sadman Shaharier Mahim's authority, 13 Nov 2026 |

On `f1ed1fe` (core-frontend after the merging-core merge) SCR-18 already shows within 3 s on `#/eva/EV1` and `#/ops`, both with the Hab Core killed and with the socket closed and every reconnect refused; the 13 Nov observation was made on a `merging-core` that did not yet carry frontend package 18 (`LiveFrame`, `useLinkLost`) and FD-545. `release-network` now asserts all four cases (EVA and ops, kill and refused socket), including SCR-18 clearing on reconnect | A focused red test could not be written because the defect no longer reproduces; the browser checks against the production build are the evidence | decided by the orchestrator (Claude) under Sadman Shaharier Mahim's authority, 13 Nov 2026 |

The layer sources keep their `attributions` from `source_ids` (`rasterLayers.ts`, `imageStatic.ts`, `MiniMap.tsx`, `OpsMap.tsx`) | Only the visual box goes (brief): with no control nothing renders them, and keeping them keeps the NFR-07 source-credit link testable on each layer.
